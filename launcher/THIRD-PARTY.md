The previous uncleared fallback is excluded from new public candidates. Historical snapshots are unchanged.

The vendored nlohmann JSON 3.11.2 header is MIT licensed; see src/utils/nlohmann-json-LICENSE.txt. It is used to read the existing Homebrew on Menu storage settings.

Direct Aroma homebrew support links wiiu-env/librpxloader and wiiu-env/libwuhbutils under LGPL-3.0. Their complete client sources, licenses and build files are included in source/dependencies. The runtime modules are supplied by the user's Aroma installation.

WiiUNX2's replacement launcher is derived from [launchiine](https://github.com/wiiu-env/launchiine), with the original copyright notices preserved. Its rendering dependency is [wiiu-env/libgui](https://github.com/wiiu-env/libgui), whose source files carry GPL notices. The complete GPL text is provided in launcher/LICENSE, also applicable to the new menu implementation and icon generator. Original upstream assets remain in the source tree with their existing provenance; WiiUNX2's new dock assets are generated from its included source.

The binaries also link devkitPro wut, FreeType, HarfBuzz, GD, PNG, JPEG, zlib, libmad, Tremor, Ogg, bzip2 and Brotli libraries. Their installed package licenses are included in the distribution's licenses directory. devkitPro supplies their corresponding source through its package/project repositories.

Research references (not shipped as part of the program):
- https://github.com/wiiu-env/MochaPayload/blob/master/source/ios_mcp/source/mcp_loadfile.c — environment-relative men.rpx replacement.
- https://github.com/wiiu-env/homebrew_on_menu_plugin/blob/main/src/main.cpp — stock-menu physical hook; excluded from the isolated environment.
- https://github.com/wiiu-env/launchiine — original replacement architecture and unresolved system integration limitations.

WiiUNX2 is an independent homebrew project. No Nintendo firmware or game artwork is included in the replacement distribution.

Version 0.5 menu music is an original procedural composition generated by `tools/Generate-Music.mjs`, under GPL-3.0-or-later. It contains no sampled Nintendo audio. The older upstream audio files remain unused.

Version 0.6 UI effects uiMove.bin, uiClick.bin and uiBack.bin are converted from the user's supplied Nintendo Switch sound folder (Select.wav, Klick.wav, Enter & Back.wav). They retain their original copyrights and are excluded from the code's GPL license. SOUND-ASSETS.json records the input hashes and format. This is a customized local build; no redistribution license for those sounds is supplied.

TVii research: https://github.com/Project-Rose/RosePatcher and dependencies/AutobootModule/source/QuickStartUtils.cpp (regional TVii IDs and SYSAPP_PFID_TVII).
